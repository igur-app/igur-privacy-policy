Privacy Policy

App: iGur

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how iGur handles information when you use our educational app. Please read it carefully.

1. Information We Handle

When you create an account, iGur asks for your full name, email address, and password. These details are used for account registration and login. Authentication is provided through Supabase.

Your password is submitted to the authentication service for account access. Do not share your password with anyone.

2. Bookmarks and Downloaded Study Materials

iGur provides features for bookmarking study materials and downloading educational PDFs. Some bookmark information is stored locally on your device. The app also accesses an online bookmarks database through Supabase. The information stored in that database depends on the relevant feature and its configuration.

Downloaded PDFs may be stored on your device so you can access them later. Their exact storage location and any associated information should be checked in the app's implementation.

3. Notifications

iGur uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to receive and handle app notifications. Firebase may process technical information, such as a messaging token, to support this service. We have not confirmed that iGur uploads this token to its own database.

4. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Create and authenticate user accounts.
  • Provide access to educational content and app features.
  • Support bookmarks and downloaded study materials.
  • Deliver notifications where enabled.
  • Respond to support requests and maintain app security.

5. Third-Party Services

iGur uses third-party services to support account authentication, online data access, and notifications. These include:

  • Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security

These services may process information as necessary to provide their functionality, subject to their own policies and applicable terms.

6. Advertising

Advertising is not currently described as an active feature of iGur. If advertising is introduced, this policy and the app's applicable privacy disclosures will be updated before the advertising feature is enabled.

7. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information used by the app. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Account and online data may remain with the relevant service until deleted or no longer needed, subject to applicable requirements. Information stored locally may remain on your device until you remove it or uninstall the app.

8. Your Choices and Data Deletion

For questions about your account or to request deletion of information associated with your account, contact us at the email address below. We may need to verify your request before taking action. Some data may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

iGur is an educational app. Its intended age group and any special protections for children must be assessed according to the app's actual audience and applicable requirements. Parents or guardians with privacy concerns may contact us using the details below.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features or data practices change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised date.
